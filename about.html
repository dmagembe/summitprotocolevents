Our foundation

Built from service, training and a clear standard.

Summit Protocol Events brings together trained protocol officers for events that need professional welcome, guest care and movement control. The team supports government, diplomatic, corporate, institutional, wedding and private events.

The company is shaped around a simple idea: the way guests are received affects how the whole event is remembered. Good protocol is visible in timing, posture, direction, discretion and warmth.
