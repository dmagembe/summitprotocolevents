Services

Event teams for welcome, order and protocol.

Full offering

Built around the guest journey.

Each service can stand alone or combine into a full front-of-house and protocol team, from arrival and registration to seating, VIP movement, programme support and departure.

Executive Ushering

Polished ushers for guest welcome, registration, seating, programme guidance and front-of-house movement.

  • Pre-event briefing and role allocation
  • Guest reception and registration support
  • Seating and programme distribution
  • Venue flow and accessibility assistance

Protocol Management

Event protocol support for government, diplomatic, institutional and high-profile corporate functions.

  • Run-of-show protocol support
  • Order of precedence guidance
  • Official guest reception
  • Security and delegation liaison

VIP Guest Handling

Discreet arrival, holding-room, seating, movement and departure support for important invited guests.

  • Dedicated VIP welcome points
  • Personal usher assignment
  • Private lounge coordination
  • Real-time guest movement support

Corporate Functions

Ushers and front-of-house teams for AGMs, conferences, launches, award ceremonies and dinners.

How we work

A simple process before event day.

The work starts before guests arrive. We clarify the brief, map the venue, assign roles and prepare the team around the programme.

1

Brief

Understand the event, guest profile, venue and expected tone.

2

Proposal

Recommend team size, roles, uniform direction and timing.

3

Prepare

Brief the team, review arrival points and confirm responsibilities.

4

Serve

Arrive early, receive guests and manage flow through the programme.

5

Review

Debrief with the client and improve the next assignment.
